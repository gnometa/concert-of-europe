## Plausibility pass, 1821.9.1 (key countries)

Table from `python scripts/audit_countries.py --key`. Notes below are historical-plausibility
judgements, not engine errors.

- **ENG** (United Kingdom; there is no GBR tag) — `hms_government`, ruling `ENG_liberal`.
  [medium] In September 1821 Britain was governed by Lord Liverpool's **Tory** ministry;
  a Whig/liberal government is a decade early. Suggest `ENG_conservative`. `yes_slavery`
  (abolished 1833) and `wealth_weighted_voting` (pre-Reform-Act) are correct.
- **FRA** — `prussian_constitutionalism2`, `FRA_conservative_6`, `landed_voting`, capital 425.
  [low] The Charte monarchy is a fair fit, but the Villèle **ultraroyalist** ministry took over in
  December 1821; `FRA_reactionary_3` (defined 1820.1.1–1830.7.1) would be more apt.
- **AUS / PRU / DEN** — `absolute_monarchy`, conservative, `none_voting`. Correct for 1821.
- **RUS** — see [high] above: `socialist_democracy` is plainly wrong for Alexander I.
- **TUR** (Ottoman Empire) — `absolute_monarchy`, reactionary, `civilized = yes`, 120 provinces,
  `yes_slavery`. Plausible; the mod treats the Ottomans as civilised, which is a design choice.
- **SPA** — `hms_government` + `SPA_liberal`: correct, this is the Trienio Liberal (1820–23).
- **POR / UPB / BRZ** — POR and BRZ own 0 provinces; the **UPB** (Portugal-Brazil) tag holds all
  99 and is `hms_government` + `POR_liberal`. Correct for the Vintista period; Brazil separates in
  September 1822. [low] POR and BRZ both keep `capital = 521`/`2447` inside UPB territory, which is
  fine for a formable but worth a comment in the files.
- **NET** — `prussian_constitutionalism`, `NET_regeringsgezinden`. Correct (United Kingdom of the
  Netherlands, 1815 constitution).
- **SWE** — `hms_government2`, conservative. Fair for the 1809 Instrument of Government.
- **SAR / PAP / SIC** (the Two Sicilies tag is **SIC**, not TWO) — absolute monarchy / theocracy,
  reactionary. Correct for the post-1821 restoration in Italy.
- **BAV** — `absolute_monarchy`. [low] Bavaria had a written constitution and a Landtag from 1818;
  `prussian_constitutionalism` fits better.
- **USA** — `democracy`, `USA_conservative`, `universal_weighted_voting`, `yes_slavery`.
  [low] Universal (weighted) franchise in 1821 is generous — property qualifications still existed
  in most states — but it is the usual mod convention. Era of Good Feelings = fine.
- **MEX** — `prussian_constitutionalism`, conservative, capital 2172, 62 provinces.
  [medium] Mexican independence was proclaimed on 27 September 1821, three weeks *after* the start
  date, and the immediate regime was Iturbide's empire; consider making MEX start as a Spanish
  possession with an event, or set `government = absolute_monarchy`.
- **GCO** — `democracy` + `GCO_liberal`, `yes_slavery`, 32 provinces. Correct: the Cúcuta congress
  sat in 1821, and abolition was only gradual (free-womb law), so `yes_slavery` is right.
- **PEU** (Peru) — `presidential_dictatorship`, 19 provinces. San Martín proclaimed independence
  in July 1821 while Spain still held the highlands; acceptable.
- **PER** (Persia) — `civilized = yes`, `absolute_monarchy3`. [medium] Qajar Persia is uncivilised
  in vanilla/PDM at 1836 and had no modern administration in 1821; `civilized = yes` here is
  a strong outlier and gives Persia full research from turn one.
- **QNG** (Qing) — `absolute_monarchy`, `civilized = no`, 151 provinces, manchu primary culture.
  Correct. **CHI** is the 0-province formable republic (`presidential_dictatorship`), also fine.
- **JAP** — `absolute_monarchy`, reactionary, uncivilised, only 3 provinces: the rest of Japan is
  held by daimyo tags (TKG 13, SAT 5, SEN 4, KAG 4, CHO 4, TOS 3, YZW 3, RYU 2). Intentional; the
  Tokugawa bakufu is TKG. No defect, but note JAP is the imperial-court tag, not the shogunate.
- **EGY** — `absolute_monarchy`, `civilized` **absent** (so uncivilised), misri culture, 34
  provinces, `yes_slavery`. Historically right for Muhammad Ali. [low] EGY is not scripted as an
  Ottoman vassal at start; check whether a `vassal_of`/sphere relation is wanted.
- **GRE** — `civilized = yes`, `absolute_monarchy`, `nv_might`, 5 provinces, capital 834 (Athens,
  owned by TUR). [medium] The Greek revolt started in March 1821 and the provisional government
  was a **republic**; there was no Greek monarchy until Otto in 1832. Suggest a
  democracy/`presidential_dictatorship` government with a liberal or conservative party, plus the
  capital fix above.
- **HAI** — `presidential_dictatorship` + `HAI_liberal` (illegal combination, see [high]).
  Boyer had just unified the island (1820/1822); 2 provinces looks low if Santo Domingo is meant to
  be Haitian only from 1822 — that is defensible.
