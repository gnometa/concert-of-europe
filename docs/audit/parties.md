## Defects

### [high] — fixed in this pass

- `CoE_RoI_R/common/countries/D33.txt:159` — party `dominion_communist` had
  `start_date = 1848.1.11860.1.1` (two dates concatenated), which the engine cannot
  parse — **fixed** to `start_date = 1848.1.1`, matching the same party in the other
  dominion files (`D01`–`D50`).

No other high-severity defects remain: every ideology name, issue key and option
value resolves, every tag in `countries.txt` has a file, every referenced file has
at least one party (`rebels.txt`/`REB` is exempt — vanilla also gives it none), and
no country has a year in 1821–1947 without an active party. `audit_parties.py`
exits 0.

### [medium] — 87 findings

- **No conservative party for part of the game (76 countries).** The engine uses a
  conservative party as its ruling-party fallback; where none is active it picks
  another ideology, which is playable but drifts from intent. Largest clusters:
  the 52 dominion files `D01`–`D50` and friends (conservative ends 1938),
  11 Middle-Eastern/minor tags (ends 1936), and one-offs such as
  `Australia.txt` (1910–1918), `Ireland.txt`-style gaps and
  `Albania.txt` (1946–1947).
  Proposed fix: extend the last conservative party's `end_date` to `2000.1.1`, the
  convention used by most files. Deferred — it changes AI government behaviour late
  game and should be a deliberate balance decision.
- **11 zero-length party windows** (`start_date` = `end_date` = `2000.1.1`), all
  outside the game window so they never activate:
  `CSA.txt:32`, `CSA.txt:46`, `Lucca.txt:74`, `Modena.txt:74`, `Parma.txt:74`,
  `Romagna.txt:74`, `Sardinia.txt:74`, `Sardinia-Piedmont.txt:74`, `Savoy.txt:74`,
  `Tuscany.txt:74`, `Two Sicilies.txt:74`.
  Proposed fix: either delete the block or give it a real window; they look like
  placeholders for parties that were meant to appear after a formation event.

### [low] — 2415 findings

- **Ideology not yet enabled at the party's `start_date` (2301 findings,
  informational).** `common/ideologies.txt` gates ideologies by `date`
  (communist 1848, socialist 1860, anarcho_liberal 1865, social_liberal 1900,
  fascist 1905) and `events/IdeologyEnabling.txt` additionally requires the
  matching invention/event. Parties whose `start_date` precedes that date simply
  stay inert until the ideology is enabled — this is normal PDM practice and needs
  no change. Counts: communist 590, anarcho_liberal 578, socialist 577,
  social_liberal 505, fascist 51.
- **101 country files not referenced by `common/countries.txt`.** Mostly
  releasable/alt-history content kept for later (US states, Australian colonies,
  Japanese han, Caribbean islands, Micronesian tags, `Sapmi`, `Karelia`,
  `Kyrgyzstan`, `Uzbekistan`, `Tataria`, `Luzica`, `Siebenburgen`, …). They are
  dead weight, not errors — the engine never loads them. Proposed fix: register the
  ones a release/formation decision needs, delete the rest.
- **12 duplicate party names within one file**, which makes two parties share a
  localisation key and appear identically in the politics UI:
  `Argentina.txt:115` (`ARG_conservative_5`), `Scotland.txt:32` (`SCO_liberal`),
  `Mewar.txt:102` (`MEW_fascist`), and the copy-pasted Indian/Ceylonese sets in
  `Dravidistan.txt` (`KRN_socialist`, `KRN_communist`, `KRN_fascist`),
  `Sri Lanka.txt` (`SRI_*`) and `Travancore.txt` (`TRA_*`).
  Proposed fix: suffix the second block (`_2`) and add the localisation key.

## Notes on method

- Dates are compared by year; a party counts as active in year *Y* if its
  `[start_date, end_date]` window overlaps *Y*.
- `REB` (`rebels.txt`) is exempt from the "no parties" check.
- Files are read as cp1252 and parsed with the tolerant tokenizer in
  `scripts/refcheck.py`, so a stray brace elsewhere in a file will not silently
  hide its parties (`modcheck.py braces` covers that separately).
